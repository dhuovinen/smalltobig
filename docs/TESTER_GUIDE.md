# Tester guide: practising small talk that leads somewhere

Thank you for trying this. For two weeks you'll use one short routine before and
after real conversations, and tell us honestly whether it helped.

## What you're testing

A four-step way to turn small talk with a stranger into a real connection:

1. **Share something honest** about yourself. A small admission, not a pitch: "I
   almost didn't come tonight."
2. **Ask about what they just said.** Pick up on their words rather than switching
   topic.
3. **Share something that connects**, about the same size as what they shared.
4. **Only then, ask or offer.** Something specific and no-pressure, such as checking
   in, an introduction, or a recommendation.

The app helps you prepare, lets you practise with a fictional person, and checks
each step afterwards. Your job is to tell us when its checks are wrong.

You'll get your own link, such as `http://100.103.52.82:8031/htmsw/profile`. It only

## The routine (about 30 minutes in all, per event)

| When | Where | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Once, at the start | **Profile:** four multiple-choice questions | 1 min |
| Before an event | **Prepare:** choose "Cold networking" and read your card | 3 min |
| Before an event | **Rehearse:** practise with Sarah, then press *End and score* | 10 min |
| At the event | Use one or two lines from the card, in your own words | — |
| Within 24 hours | **Debrief:** write a few sentences about what happened | 5 min |
| Every time | **Feedback:** the short form at the end of each page | 1 min |

**Over the two weeks, please aim for:**
- at least 3 rehearsals with Sarah;
- at least 2 real conversations, each debriefed within a day.

Any event counts: a meetup, a school event, a neighbour, a conference coffee break.

## The feedback form

The form is the most useful thing you can give us.

- **"Was the judge right?"** For each step, say whether you agree with its yes or
  not yet. If it gave you credit you didn't earn, or missed something you did, mark
  it wrong.
- **"Was this useful?"** Yes, somewhat, or no.
- **"Anything get in the way?"** Tick what applies.

Please be blunt. Agreeing with a judge that was wrong makes the results worse, not
better.

## What is stored

Your link runs one of two settings. We'll tell you which, and you'll always see it
at the top of the page.

- **abstracted** (the default): we keep only yes/no results and your form answers,
  as numbers. Nothing you type is saved: no transcript, no debrief text, no names.
- **full** (only if you've agreed): we also keep your rehearsal transcripts, debrief
  text and comments, so we can see why the judge got something wrong.

Please don't write anyone's full name or other identifying details in a debrief.
First names or "a woman at the meetup" are plenty. Before writing about a recording
of a conversation, choose the consent option that applies: some places, Florida for
example, require everyone's consent to record.

To delete everything stored on your link, use **Purge** in the menu.
